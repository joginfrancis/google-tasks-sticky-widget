Proposed — click the tasks

Click a row to peek. Hover for tools. Click a date chip for the calendar — or use the one in the hover tools. Subtasks stay as rows, because you drag them. Esc closes.

    Ladder — click, click, click

    One click: the essentials. Click again: subtasks and the full controls. Click once more: the page window. The row says what the next click will do.
